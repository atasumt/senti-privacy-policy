Data Deletion Instructions — SENTI

Last updated: December 2025

According to Facebook Platform rules, users must be able to request deletion of the data collected by the SENTI mobile application.

1. How to Request Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us at:

atasumt@gmail.com

Please include the following information in your request:

  • Your registered email address
  • If you logged in with Facebook, your Facebook account email
  • Statement requesting deletion of all data

2. What Will Be Deleted

Upon receiving your deletion request, we will delete the following data from our systems:

  • Authentication information
  • Email and profile information provided by Facebook, Google or Apple
  • Analytics identifiers (Firebase Analytics)
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

3. Processing Time

Your request will be processed within up to 7 business days.

You will receive a confirmation message once your data has been permanently removed.
